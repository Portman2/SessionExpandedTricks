# Expanded Tricks 1.1.0: the trick list

Every trick the mod adds, with how to do it on the pad. The short table is in the [README](../README.md#controls).

## Which stick is which

The game has three stick modes (Options > Gameplay). The mod works in two of them.

- **Front foot / back foot:** the left stick is always your front foot and the right stick your back foot, in every
  stance. Riding goofy, left and right on the sticks swap as they do for the game's own kickflip.
- **Left foot / right foot:** each stick is that foot. The back-foot stick is the right stick for a regular rider
  riding forward and for a goofy rider riding switch. It is the left stick for a goofy rider riding forward and for a
  regular rider riding switch.
- **Legacy:** the grabs, hippie jumps and the rest work, but the alternate flip set is off.

Below, *front stick* and *back stick* mean the stick of your front foot and the stick of your back foot. For a regular
rider riding forward that is the left and the right stick in both modes.

*Toe side* and *heel side* are the sides of the board. The toe side is on the right for a regular rider rolling
forward, on the left for a goofy one, and the other way round riding switch. *Your heelflip's side* is the way you
flick for the game's heelflip, and *your kickflip's side* the way you flick for its kickflip.

The tricks keep your stance. Done riding switch they are the switch versions. The nollie set gives the nollie versions,
and the fakie versions when you ride switch. Each shows its own name in the trick display.

## The alternate flip set

Needs Epic's exe and the front foot / back foot or left foot / right foot stick mode. *Alt flip set* and
*Alt nollie set* on the Flip Tricks page switch the two crouches on or off.

**The alternate crouch:** from a centred stick, push the **back stick up** (within about 40 degrees of straight up, at
least half way) and hold it. The skater crouches as for an ollie. Let the stick back to the middle to end the crouch.
After an alternate trick it must come back to the middle once before the next one. A stick coming up out of the game's
own crouch is not taken (a bone ollie stays a bone ollie).

| Trick | From the alternate crouch | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Monster Flip | front stick **down** | The board turns a full circle end over end, nose first, under you. With the game's catch set to manual it keeps turning until you catch it (flick either stick as it comes round level); two turns are a Double Monster Flip. It needs about 45 cm under the board before it turns, or it stays an ollie. |
| Dolphin Flip | front stick **up** | End over end forward, then a half flip the kickflip's way. Lands reversed. |
| Porpoise Flip | front stick **up, leaning toward your heelflip's side** | The dolphin with the half flip the heelflip's way. Lands reversed. *Porpoise flip: lean* sets how far you lean. |
| Nightmare Flip | your **varial flip's** scoop (the back stick from up, round toward the varial's side) and flick | A 180 shove-it with a double kickflip. |
| Daydream Flip | your **varial heelflip's** scoop and flick | A 180 shove-it with a double heelflip. |
| Kickflip Underflip | your **kickflip's** flick, then **either stick sideways, either way**, while the board is upside down | The board turns back the way it came, from underneath, and lands grip up. The flicking foot meets the board's edge from below. |
| Heelflip Cancel Flip | your **heelflip's** flick, then either stick sideways while the board is upside down | The same out of a heelflip: the back foot pushes the upside-down edge back down. |
| Shuv-it Underflip | a shove-it (a **quarter scoop** of the back stick), then a **late sideways flick** of either stick | The shove-it spins 180 and the board does a whole flip. The foot that shoves does the underflip. Backside: the kickflip's roll, flicked toward your **heelflip's** side (regular, forward: right). Frontside: the heelflip's roll, flicked toward your **kickflip's** side (regular, forward: left). A flick the other way leaves the game's own shove-it. |
| Shuv-it Under Varial Flip | a **half scoop** (the back stick rolled at least 135 degrees round, the 360 shove-it's scoop), then the late flick | The shove-it spins 360 and the flip finishes with the spin, as one varial flip. |

Anything else you do from the crouch is the game's own trick.

**The alternate nollie crouch:** from a centred stick, pull the **front stick down** and hold it. Then the back stick:

| Trick | Then | Riding switch |
|---|---|---|
| Nollie Monster Flip | back stick **down** | Fakie Monster Flip |
| Nollie Dolphin Flip | back stick **up** | Fakie Dolphin Flip |
| Nollie Porpoise Flip | back stick up, leaning toward your heelflip's side | Fakie Porpoise Flip |
| Nollie Nightmare Flip | your nollie varial flip's scoop and flick | Fakie Nightmare Flip |
| Nollie Daydream Flip | your nollie varial heelflip's scoop and flick | Fakie Daydream Flip |
| Nollie Kickflip Underflip / Heelflip Cancel Flip | your nollie kickflip's or heelflip's flick, then either stick sideways while upside down | Fakie ... |
| Nollie Shuv-it Underflip / Under Varial Flip | the nollie shove-it's scoop, then the late flick | Fakie ... |

The nollie monster and dolphin flip end over end the other way from the ollie ones. On a nollie shove-it the front
foot shoves, so the pairing of backside / frontside with the flick side is mirrored.

## Hospital Flip

1. Switch on **Caspers** in the game's own experimental settings.
2. Do a **kickflip** (the game's own, or the one from the alternate crouch) and catch it in a **casper** in the air,
   the game's way.
3. Still in the air, pull the **front stick back** (down).

The board turns 180 end over end on your front foot, scooping a little sideways, and lands grip up and reversed. Out of
the alternate crouch's kickflip the back foot stays off the board, hovering over the tail, as in a real hospital flip.
Only out of a kickflip's casper (not a heelflip's), and only in the air: on the ground the game's own way out of the
casper stays. *Hospital flip: time* on the Flip Tricks page sets how long the 180 takes (higher = slower). Riding
switch, nollie or fakie it is the Switch, Nollie or Fakie Hospital Flip. It is part of the alternate flip set: it
needs *Alt flip set* on, Epic's exe, and the front foot / back foot or left foot / right foot stick mode.

## Wallrides

Needs Epic's exe. The Wallrides page has the settings.

- **Ollie-in:** in any air on the board (an ollie, or a flip once it has spun), fly into a wall at a shallow angle:
  60 degrees or less between your way of travel and the wall, at 300 cm/s (about 11 km/h) or more, with the board at
  least 40 cm off the ground. You ride along the wall for up to a second, the wheels rolling on it with their sound.
  The ride ends where the wall ends, near the ground or when you slow down. You ride down, the board rolls flat and you
  land turned away from the wall. A head-on hit still bails you, as in the game.
- **Backside and frontside:** with your back to the wall the body takes the game's own unused wallride animation.
  Facing the wall it is your riding pose leaned out from it. *Wallride: frontside walls too* switches frontside off.
- **Pop off:** push a stick up during the ride to pop off, turned away from the wall (not while you crouch or grab).
- **Curved walls:** a ride follows a curve instead of taking it for a corner.
- **From the ground (ride-on):** *Wallride from the ground* (Wallrides page) is on by default. Roll into a
  wall at a shallow angle, fast enough, and **hold the crouch** (the ollie's crouch) as you reach it. The board does a
  wallie, the nose coming up, and rolls up onto the wall into a wallride, with no ollie. Rolling in standing up is the
  game's own crash. It is not taken at benches, ledges, curbs or ramps, while you push, manual or powerslide, or twice
  at one wall.

## Caveman

On foot with the board in your hand, jump (**X**), then press **Y** in the air. The board swings from your hand, out in
front and down, to under your feet. The hand holds it with the knees up (a Melon with the front hand or an Indy with
the back hand), lets go before you land, and you roll away. Your fall keeps its speed.

**Switch caveman:** tap **Y twice** quickly in the air: the feet go onto the switch side and you land switch.

The caveman's settings are on the Hippie Jumps page.

## Hippie jumps

- **Hippie jump:** hold one stick down to crouch, then click the **other** stick (L3 or R3). You jump off the board, it
  rolls on under you, and you land back on it. *Hippie jump height* sets how high.
- **180 / 360:** hold **LT** or **RT** in the air to spin your body above the board. A half turn is a 180 and lands
  you switch with the board as it was. A full turn is a 360. Landing far from a clean half or full turn bails you
  (*Bail on a bad rotation* turns that off). *Swap the rotation triggers* swaps which trigger spins which way.
- **Switch hippie jumps:** the same while riding switch. A switch 180 lands you back in your own stance.
- **Mini hippie jump:** click **both sticks** (L3 + R3) at once. No crouch is needed.
- The camera follows the board's travel through a 180's landing instead of swinging round.
- *Hippie jumps* on the Hippie Jumps page picks the animation: 1 the jump, 2 the game's ollie, 3 one or the other at
  random, 0 off.

## No comply, boneless and bean plant

- **No comply:** the pressure kickflip's input. Riding regular: **left stick down + right stick left**. Goofy and
  switch follow the game's own mapping for that trick. Your front foot steps off and plants on the ground as the
  board pops, and the board comes back up under you. The game catches it for you.
- **Boneless:** hold **RB** (back hand) while rolling, then do the no comply. The hand is on the board through the
  trick and lets go by itself as the board comes back under your feet.
- **Bean plant:** the same with **LB** (front hand).
- All three work regular and goofy, riding forward and switch. Let the bumper go before your next grab. A bumper
  pressed late in the trick puts no hand on.

## Grabs

- **One-hand grabs:** in the air, hold **LB** for the front hand (the left stick picks the grab) or **RB** for the back
  hand (the right stick picks). Push toward your toes for Mute (front hand) or Indy (back hand), toward your heels for
  Melon or Stalefish. Up and down give the nose and tail grabs. Let the bumper go to let go. The game's own twelve
  grabs; the four between the legs (Chicken Salad, Taipan, Roast Beef, Canadian Bacon) are off as the mod ships
  (`GrabBlocked=None` in the ini brings them back).
- **Two-hand grabs:** hold **LB and RB** together. The two grabs your sticks point at make the pair: Cannonball (left
  stick up, right stick down), Quasimodo (both sticks up), Gorilla (no stick, or both toward your toes), Truck Driver
  (front hand's stick toward your heels, back hand's toward your toes), Drunk Driver (the other way round) and Squirrel
  (both toward your heels).
- **Tweaks:** move the stick off the direction it picked the grab with. *Grab tweak* on the Grabs page picks rotating
  (your legs and the board twist) or boning (the board is poked out toward the nose and your back leans to keep the
  hand on). In a Gorilla, Truck Driver, Drunk Driver or Squirrel, push both sticks up after the grab is made to bone
  it.
- **Rolling grabs:** riding on the ground, hold **RB** to put your back hand on the toe edge between your feet, or
  **LB** for the front hand. The hand stays on while the bumper is held, through an ollie, a drop or a no comply.
- **Finger flips:** flick a flip trick with a rolling grab's hand on the board. The hand lets go as the board leaves
  the ground, so it looks as if the hand flipped it. *Rolling grab: flips allowed* picks which flips can be flicked.
- In the left foot / right foot stick mode **LB is the left hand and RB the right**, as the sticks are the left and the
  right foot. The two bumpers change places whenever your left foot is at the back (a regular rider riding switch, a
  goofy rider riding forward), for every grab, the rolling grabs, the boneless and the bean plant.

## Drop-ins, the throwdown and getting on the board

- **Drop-in:** on foot at the edge of a ledge, bank or ramp (within about 90 cm) with the board in your hand, press
  **Y**. The board is set tail-down on the coping and you step on. Push a stick **up** to drop in, **LB** or **RB** to
  step back onto the ledge, **Y** to step off. Off a ramp the board tips down onto its face; off a plain ledge or wall
  you roll straight out; on a vert wall the speed you get goes with the wall's height. Your arms come out for balance
  and you crouch on the way down, both by how big the drop is; holding the crouch stick takes you lower.
- **Drop-in in switch:** tap **Y twice** at the edge: you are put on facing away from the drop.
- **Throwdown:** farther from an edge, Y is the game's own throwdown (Y twice for its switch throwdown).
  *Throwdown speed* on the General page plays it faster and sends you off faster, as a percentage of the speed you were
  moving at. Standing still, nothing is added.
- **Jumping onto the board:** on foot next to your board lying on the ground (after a bail, for example), click a stick
  (**L3** or **R3**). You jump onto it and ride away the way you face. Run at it and your speed is carried onto the
  board.

## Other things it changes

- **Left stick steers the board** (General page): on = the game's own steering. Off = only the triggers turn you, so a
  stick trick no longer pulls the board off its line.
- **Max speed** and **Push strength** (General page): percentages of the game's own top speed and push. Both ship at
  100 %.
- **Stick clicks:** a click the mod uses (a hippie jump, a mini hop, a jump onto the board) is kept from the game, so
  L3 no longer opens the missions every time. Any other click goes on to the game.
- **Powerslides after a hippie jump:** the game sometimes left its crouch half started after a hop, which blocked the
  next powerslide. The mod finishes it after a second.
- **Legs:** the shins that the game's animations warp above the ankle are straightened in hippie jumps, no complies,
  bonelesses, bean plants and wallrides.
